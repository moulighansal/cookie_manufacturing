Questions, answered

From ingredients and production volumes to packaging and private-label programs, here is what partners usually want to know before getting started.

01

Manufacturing

What types of cookies can you manufacture?
We manufacture a range of commercial cookie formats, from classic chocolate chip and butter-forward recipes to specialty and custom-developed products.
Can you manufacture custom recipes?
Yes. Production programs can be developed around partner requirements, including recipe specifications, product formats and target characteristics.
What production volumes can you support?
Our manufacturing approach is designed for dependable commercial-scale production while maintaining consistent product quality across production runs.
Do you offer private-label manufacturing?
Yes. We support private-label programs for businesses looking for a manufacturing partner from production through finished-product supply.
02

Quality

How do you maintain consistency?
Controlled processes, documented production procedures and quality checks help ensure that every production run delivers dependable results.
How are ingredients handled?
Ingredients are handled through controlled production processes designed to maintain consistency, food safety and product quality.
Do you conduct quality checks during production?
Quality is considered throughout the manufacturing process, from incoming ingredients through production, cooling, inspection and final packaging.
03

Supply & Packaging

Can you handle packaging as well?
Yes. Finished-product programs can include packaging support so products are prepared for dependable commercial supply.
Can you support recurring commercial orders?
Our production model is built around repeatable manufacturing and dependable supply for commercial partners.
How do we start a manufacturing enquiry?
Start by sharing your product requirements, expected volumes, packaging needs and timeline. Our team can then review the opportunity and discuss the next steps.
